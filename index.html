Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a planning fee?

Contact Breezy for the current service and fee policy. Any applicable planning fees should be disclosed before you agree to services.

Can I transfer an existing booking?

Possibly. Transfer eligibility and deadlines depend on the cruise line, booking terms and whether the booking has been paid in full.

What travel documents do I need?

Requirements depend on citizenship, destination and itinerary. A valid passport is generally recommended. Check official government guidance and your cruise line's current requirements before travel.

Are advertised prices and offers guaranteed?

No. Availability, fares and promotions can change. Only a verified supplier quote and booking confirmation establish your actual terms.
